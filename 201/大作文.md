## 2. 第二段

- 自由发挥，没有技巧

承接上下文
- Nowadays, ...
- For one thing, ...
- For another thing, ...

常用句型，具体考试随意组合：

1.   so ... that

     Smartphones are **so** convenient **that** people can hardly live without them.

2.   not only ... but also

     **Not only** does social media waste time, **but it also** increases comparison and anxiety.

3.   It is ... that ... 

     **It is** social pressure **that** drives many students to study late.

4.   As time went by ... 

     **As time went by**, traditional family ties became weaker.

5.   One factor stands out -- xxx

     One factor stands out — the rapid development of technology.

6.   The more... the more...

     **The more** people compare themselves with others, **the more** anxious they become.

7.   A possible explanation is that...

     **A possible explanation is that** people are busier than before.
