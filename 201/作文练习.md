As is vividly depicted in the pictures, while a teenager writes the name of Beckham on his face, trying to show how much he loves the famous football player, there is another man who spent three hundred yuan on changing his hairstyle into a weird and ugly one. From this ridiculous phenomenon, it can be inferred that a lot of people, especially young people, blindly follow fashion, which deserves our reflection and discussion.

It cannot be denied that the pursuit of fashion is common. Actually, it is a lucrative business for big companies, luring consumers by hyping new styles over and over again, and people seem happy to contribute their time and money to support their favorite brands. In addition, the government sometimes chooses to stand by for economic advancement. Another serious problem is most people don't have the ability to discern the fashion traps created by capital. It is a shame to admit that our education only pursues scores and neglects teenagers' values, which makes a lot of people lose their minds in these meaningless things.

All in all, it should be pointed out that there is no correct way to chase stars and fashion. All the benefits of pursuing these things are false and deceptive. We should emulate the author of Walden -- escape the illusions created by capital and find the truth of our lives. Only in this way can we have a better future.
