As is vividly depicted in this picture, a primary student is embracing his AI-powered maid with a smile. "The reason why I study and learning is to create you", the boy says. The picture aims to convey a thought-provoking message that although artificial intelligence is developing at an unimaginable speed, knowledge remains important and indispensable.

There is no denying that AI brought a lot of convenience and advance to our lives. In fact, it can process data, solve math problem and coding program, even sometimes it can finish better than human. Consequently, some people worried about that human being will be replaced by AI and hold the idea that acquiring knowledge is a waste of time. However, we always neglect that this statement is the hype of AI companies and there is still a clear gap between AI and human being in many fields. Without sufficient knowledge, critical thinking and creativity, people can't unleash the potential of AI. For example, it is impossible to call a normal primary student to drive AI tools to launch a cyber attack, unless it is another hype by AI companies.
