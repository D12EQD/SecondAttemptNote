## 0. 称呼 (Salutation)

-   **只知道姓：** Dear Mr. Liu / Dear Ms. Mo
-   **只知道名：** Dear Ping
-   **只知道职位：** Dear Manager / Dear President / Dear Editor / Dear Professor
-   **都不知道：** Dear Sir / Dear Madam / Dear Friends

## 1. 第一段 (Opening Paragraph)

简要概括背景提要 + 来信意图

### 1.1. 背景提要 (Background)

#### 1.1.1. 私人书信

-   I hope this letter/email finds you well and ...
-   It was a great pleasure to receive your email.
-   I want to express my [feeling] through this letter regarding [topic] that ...

#### 1.1.2. 公务书信

-   I am an undergraduate majoring in computer science and technology.
-   I am the [president / senior executive] of [the Student Union / a technology company].

### 1.2. 来信意图 (Purpose)

-   I am writing this letter in order to ...
-   express my gratitude / appreciate your...
-   clarify some misunderstandings / apologize for my...
-   suggest some improvements / make several suggestions about...
-   complain about...
-   praise / commend your excellent customer service
-   invite you to participate in / attend...
-   inform you that... / notify you of the meeting
-   inquire about...
-   recommend... to you / introduce myself
-   request your assistance
-   offer my support
-   apply for a position as an assistant
-   request to resign from my present position
-   describe my recent plan / provide further details about the plan
-   specify the event arrangement / outline my daily schedule
-   confirm our appointment
-   summarize the main points of the meeting
-   highlight the importance of this matter

## 2. 第二段 (Body Paragraph)

过渡句 + 自由句

### 2.1. 过渡句 (Transition Sentences)

-   To shed light on this matter, the specific [something] **are** outlined below. *(为了阐明此事，具体的……概述如下)*

**更加具体的场景：**

-   **劝告：** To help you out **of a** dilemma, several constructive suggestions are put forward as follows.
-   **投诉：** To voice my deep discontent, the specific problems **I** encountered are listed as follows.
-   **邀请：** To let you know more information about the upcoming event, the relevant arrangements are as follows.
-   **道歉：** To explain why I failed to show up on time, the actual causes **are presented** as follows.
-   **感谢：** To express my heartfelt thanks **for** your timely help, **here** is my appreciation for your kindness.
-   **申请职位：** To demonstrate my competence for this position, my major strengths **are listed** as follows.
-   **辞职：** The primary **considerations** behind my decision to resign **are listed** as follows.

### 2.2. 自由句 (Free Sentences)

*(没有具体题目型号，自行组织语言)*

**活动相关：**

-   The activity is scheduled to take place in the conference center on Friday from 7:00 to 8:00 p.m.
-   This meeting aims to promote in-depth discussions on something.
-   All students from all grades are eligible to enter the competition, including both domestic and international students.
-   The event is anticipated to attract wide participation from both students and faculty members.
-   During the event, you will have the opportunity to meet like-minded people, participate in interesting activities, and **spend** a pleasant time together.

**推荐：**

-   Ping Liu, an ancient Chinese scientist, was an outstanding scientist during the Tang Dynasty, who achieved remarkable accomplishments in many fields.
-   I would highly recommend [something] for its exceptional [trait] and remarkable [trait]. I believe that **it** will provide you with a more profound and thought-provoking experience.

**能力展示：**

-   Over the past few years, I **have spent** a lot of time **on** computer science and technology, which makes my **thinking** more agile and my analysis more prominent.

**通用：**

-   It is wise to do [something], which can help you complete [something].

## 3. 第三段 (Closing Paragraph)

一个自由句 + 期望回信

-   I would greatly appreciate it if you could reply at your earliest convenience.
-   [Something] provides us with a unique opportunity for creative self-exploration.
